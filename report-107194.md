# **High-Speed Rail from Lisbon to Tallinn: Track Gauge Differences and Variable-Gauge Bogie Technology (2026)**

## **Executive Summary**
Building a high-speed rail (HSR) line from **Lisbon, Portugal (1,668 mm Iberian gauge)** to **Tallinn, Estonia (1,520 mm Russian gauge)** requires traversing **five distinct track gauges** across **nine countries**. As of **August 2026**, the route encounters the following gauge variations:

| **Country**       | **Primary Track Gauge (mm)** | **Notes**                                                                                     | **[Source](https://jakubmarian.com/track-gauge-by-country-in-europe)** |
|-------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Portugal**      | 1,668                       | Iberian gauge; high-speed lines (e.g., Lisbon–Madrid) use **1,435 mm** with variable-gauge trains. | [Jakub Marian](https://jakubmarian.com/track-gauge-by-country-in-europe) |
| **Spain**         | 1,668 (conventional) / 1,435 (HSR) | **Dual-gauge adaptation** (Talgo, CAF Brava) enables seamless transitions at Irun/Port-Bou.   | [Wikipedia (Iberian Gauge)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian-gauge_railway) |
| **France**        | 1,435                       | Standard gauge; **no gauge changes** on HSR lines (LGV).                                     | [Jakub Marian](https://jakubmarian.com/track-gauge-by-country-in-europe) |
| **Belgium**       | 1,435                       | Standard gauge.                                                                              | [Jakub Marian](https://jakubmarian.com/track-gauge-by-country-in-europe) |
| **Germany**       | 1,435                       | Standard gauge.                                                                              | [Jakub Marian](https://jakubmarian.com/track-gauge-by-country-in-europe) |
| **Poland**        | 1,435                       | Standard gauge; **Rail Baltica** will connect via **Suwałki–Kaunas** (2030).                 | [Rail Baltica Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_Baltica) |
| **Lithuania**     | 1,520 (legacy) / **1,435 (Rail Baltica)** | **Mixed-gauge sections** (e.g., Kaunas–Šeštokai) until full Rail Baltica completion.            | [Rail Baltica Progress](https://www.railbaltica.org/progress-today) |
| **Latvia**        | 1,520 (legacy) / **1,435 (Rail Baltica)** | **1,435 mm corridor** under construction (Riga–Pärnu–Tallinn).                               | [RB Latvia](https://www.railbaltica.org/progress-today) |
| **Estonia**       | 1,520 (legacy) / **1,435 (Rail Baltica)** | **First 1,435 mm tracks** laid (Ülemiste terminal); full conversion by **2030**.               | [RB Estonia](https://www.railbaltica.org/progress-today) |

### **Key Findings**
1. **Variable-Gauge Bogie Technology is Mandatory**
   - **Talgo RD** and **CAF Brava** systems enable **high-speed (>250 km/h) gauge adaptation** between **1,668 mm (Iberian) ↔ 1,435 mm (Standard)** and **1,520 mm (Russian) ↔ 1,435 mm**.
   - **Gauge-changing facilities** (e.g., Irun/Hendaye, Port-Bou) allow **passenger trains to transition without stops** (Talgo AVRIL: **330 km/h** on standard gauge, **220 km/h** on Iberian gauge) ([Talgo](https://www.talgo.com/en/high-speed)).

2. **Rail Baltica: The Critical Link**
   - **2030 completion target**: A **new 1,435 mm standard-gauge line** (Tallinn–Riga–Kaunas–Warsaw) will eliminate the **1,520 mm bottleneck** in the Baltics.
   - **Phased implementation**: As of **August 2026**, **40% of the 870 km corridor is under construction**, with **first tracks laid in Lithuania** ([Rail Baltica Progress](https://www.railbaltica.org/progress-today)).

3. **Regulatory and Technical Challenges**
   - **EU TSIs (Technical Specifications for Interoperability)** mandate **variable-gauge bogies** for cross-border HSR, but **no TSI-certified system yet handles all three gauges (1,668/1,520/1,435 mm)**.
   - **Bilateral agreements** (e.g., Spain–France, Poland–Lithuania) govern gauge-changing operations, with **ERTMS Level 2 signalling** required for **>250 km/h** ([EU Commission](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/document/download/774e79c9-1ece-4514-8f16-a2b98049c82e_en)).

4. **Operational Case Studies (2026)**
   - **Spain–France**: Talgo AVRIL trains (**380 km/h capability**) operate **Paris–Barcelona–Madrid** using **variable-gauge axles** ([Talgo AVRIL](https://www.talgo.com/en/high-speed)).
   - **Poland–Lithuania**: **No direct variable-gauge HSR yet**, but **freight bogie exchanges** (SUW 2000) occur at **Mockava/Šeštokai** (speed: **30 km/h** during gauge change) ([Railway Supply](https://www.railway.supply/variable-gauge-axle-systems)).

5. **Future Outlook**
   - **2027–2030**: Rail Baltica’s **standard-gauge corridor** will enable **direct Lisbon–Tallinn HSR** via **variable-gauge trains** (e.g., Talgo or CAF).
   - **2030+**: **Full mixed-gauge infrastructure** in the Baltics may allow **legacy 1,520 mm services** to coexist with **1,435 mm HSR**.

## **2. Variable-Gauge Bogie Technology: Solutions for Seamless Travel**

### **2.1 Key Technologies**
To enable **cross-gauge high-speed rail**, two **variable-gauge bogie systems** are commercially viable in 2026:

| **Technology**       | **Developer** | **Max Speed** | **Gauge Adaptation**          | **Gauge-Change Speed** | **Operational Since** | **[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_gauge)** |
|----------------------|---------------|---------------|-------------------------------|------------------------|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Talgo RD**         | Talgo (Spain) | **330 km/h** (standard) / **200 km/h** (Iberian) | **1,668 ↔ 1,435 mm**          | **15 km/h** (24 sec/train) | **1969**             | [Talgo Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talgo) |
| **CAF Brava**        | CAF (Spain)   | **250 km/h**  | **1,668 ↔ 1,435 mm**          | **3 sec/wheelset**     | **2010**             | [CAF Brava](https://www.caf.net/en/oferta/sistemas-de-cambio-de-ancho/brava/) |

#### **How Variable-Gauge Bogies Work**
1. **Unlocking**: Wheelsets **release locks** when entering a gauge-changing facility.
2. **Gauge Adjustment**: **Hydraulic/pneumatic actuators** shift wheels **inward/outward** to match the new gauge.
3. **Relocking**: Wheels **re-lock** before exiting the facility.
   - **Talgo RD**: Uses **independent wheels** (no fixed axles), enabling **smoother transitions**.
   - **CAF Brava**: Uses **sliding axles** with **3-second adaptation** (faster than Talgo).
   - **[Technical Animation (Talgo)](https://www.talgo.com/en/technology/variable-gauge-axles)**.

### **2.2 High-Speed Compatibility (>250 km/h)**
| **Technology** | **Max Speed (Standard Gauge)** | **Max Speed (Iberian/Russian Gauge)** | **TSI Certification (2026)?** | **[Source](https://www.certifer.eu/no/achievement/talgos-high-speed-train-certification)** |
|----------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| **Talgo AVRIL** | **380 km/h**                   | **220 km/h**                         | **Yes (Spain/France)**        | [CERTIFER](https://www.certifer.eu/no/achievement/talgos-high-speed-train-certification) |
| **CAF Oaris**   | **320 km/h**                   | **160 km/h**                         | **Partial (Iberian only)**    | [CAF Oaris](https://www.caf.net/en/productos/alta-velocidad/oaris/) |

- **EU TSI Compliance**: As of **2026**, **no variable-gauge bogie is TSI-certified for all three gauges (1,668/1,520/1,435 mm)**. **Talgo and CAF** are working with **CEN/CENELEC** to standardize systems ([CEN News](https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2025/brief-news/2025-02-14-advancing-railways-standardization)).

### **2.3 Operational Case Studies**
#### **A. Talgo AVRIL (Spain–France)**
- **Route**: **Madrid–Barcelona–Perpignan–Paris**.
- **Gauge Change**: **Irun (1,668 ↔ 1,435 mm)**.
- **Speed**: **330 km/h (standard) / 220 km/h (Iberian)**.
- **Adaptation Time**: **24 seconds** at **15 km/h** ([Talgo](https://www.talgo.com/en/high-speed)).

#### **B. Rail Baltica (Future Outlook)**
- **Route**: **Warsaw–Kaunas–Riga–Tallinn (1,435 mm)**.
- **Gauge Change**: **None after 2030** (fully standard gauge).
- **Speed**: **249 km/h (passenger) / 120 km/h (freight)** ([Rail Baltica Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_Baltica)).

#### **C. SUW 2000 (Poland–Lithuania, Freight Only)**
- **Development**: Polish **SUW 2000** bogies allow **1,435 ↔ 1,520 mm** adaptation.
- **Speed During Change**: **30 km/h**.
- **Limitations**: **Not approved for passenger services** (>160 km/h).
  - **[Source](https://www.railway.supply/variable-gauge-axle-systems)**.

## **3. Regulatory and Standardization Challenges**

### **3.1 EU Technical Specifications for Interoperability (TSIs)**
The **EU Agency for Railways (ERA)** mandates compliance with **TSIs** for **cross-border rail**. Key TSIs affecting gauge adaptation:

| **TSI**               | **Scope**                          | **Status (2026)**                                                                 | **[Source](https://www.era.europa.eu/system/files/2022-11/Analysis%201520%20RST.%20Locomotives%20%20and%20multiple%20units%20%28EN%29.pdf)** |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Control-Command**   | ERTMS/ETCS Level 2 (mandatory for >250 km/h) | **Rail Baltica fully compliant**; Iberian HSR partially compliant.               | [ERA TSI](https://www.era.europa.eu/system/files/2022-11/Analysis%201520%20RST.%20Locomotives%20%20and%20multiple%20units%20%28EN%29.pdf) |
| **Rolling Stock**     | Bogie compatibility, crashworthiness | **No TSI for 1,668/1,520/1,435 mm variable-gauge bogies**. Talgo/CAF certified under **national rules**. | [ERA Rolling Stock](https://www.era.europa.eu/system/files/2022-11/Analysis%201520%20RST.%20Locomotives%20%20and%20multiple%20units%20%28EN%29.pdf) |
| **Infrastructure**    | Track gauge, electrification (25 kV) | **Rail Baltica: SE-C loading gauge, 25 kV AC**; Iberian HSR: **1x25 kV AC**.     | [Rail Baltica Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_Baltica) |

### **3.2 Bilateral Agreements**
- **Spain–France**: **Talgo trains** operate under **bilateral agreements** (no EU-wide TSI for Iberian standard transitions) ([UIC Code](https://www.scribd.com/document/804420505/e503)).
- **Poland–Lithuania**: **SUW 2000** freight bogies approved under **bilateral MoU** (no passenger certification) ([Railway Supply](https://www.railway.supply/variable-gauge-axle-systems)).

### **3.3 Ongoing Standardization Efforts**
- **CEN/CENELEC**: Working on **TSI amendments for variable-gauge rolling stock** ([CEN News](https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2025/brief-news/2025-02-14-advancing-railways-standardization)).
- **EU High-Speed Rail Plan (2026)**: Calls for **"next-generation rolling stock"** with **multi-gauge capability** ([EU Commission](https://www.railwaypro.com/wp/eu-high-speed-rail-plan-unveiled)).

## **5. Key Takeaways and Strategic Recommendations**

### **5.1 Key Takeaways**
✅ **Five gauges, two critical transitions**:
   - **Portugal (1,668 mm) → Spain/France (1,435 mm)** (solved via Talgo/CAF).
   - **Poland (1,435 mm) → Baltics (1,520 mm)** (solved via Rail Baltica after 2030).

✅ **Variable-gauge bogies are commercially viable**:
   - **Talgo RD** (330 km/h standard, 200 km/h Iberian) and **CAF Brava** (250 km/h) enable **seamless cross-border HSR**.
   - **No system yet handles all three gauges (1,668/1,520/1,435 mm)**.

✅ **Rail Baltica is the linchpin**:
   - **2030 completion** will eliminate **Baltic gauge breaks**, enabling **direct Lisbon–Tallinn HSR**.
   - **As of 2026, 40% of the 870 km corridor is under construction**.

✅ **Regulatory gaps persist**:
   - **No EU-wide TSI for variable-gauge high-speed trains**.
   - **Bilateral agreements** (Spain–France, Poland–Lithuania) govern operations.

### **5.2 Strategic Recommendations**
#### **For Operators (Train Manufacturers & Rail Companies)**
1. **Adopt Talgo AVRIL or CAF Oaris** for **Iberian ↔ Standard** transitions (**300+ km/h**).
   - **[Talgo AVRIL Specs](https://www.talgo.com/en/high-speed)**.
   - **[CAF Oaris](https://www.caf.net/en/productos/alta-velocidad/oaris/)**.
2. **Lobby for EU TSI certification** of **multi-gauge bogies** (1,668/1,520/1,435 mm) to enable **pan-European HSR**.
   - **[CEN/CENELEC Standardization](https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2025/brief-news/2025-02-14-advancing-railways-standardization)**.
3. **Develop hybrid bogies** for **freight trains** to reduce transshipment costs (e.g., **OGI variable-gauge freight bogies**).
   - **[OGI Freight Bogie](https://www.railjournal.com/technology/ogi-variable-gauge-freight-bogie-completes-first-testing-phase)**.

#### **For Policymakers (EU & National Governments)**
1. **Accelerate Rail Baltica construction** to **meet 2030 deadline** and eliminate **1,520 mm bottleneck**.
   - **[RB Funding](https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/featured-projects/rail-baltica-connecting-baltic-states-rest-europe-high-speed-train_en)**.
2. **Harmonize TSIs** for **variable-gauge rolling stock** to enable **cross-border HSR >250 km/h**.
   - **[EU High-Speed Rail Plan (2026)](https://www.railwaypro.com/wp/eu-high-speed-rail-plan-unveiled)**.
3. **Fund R&D for 1,520 ↔ 1,668 mm transitions** to enable **future Iberian–Baltic freight corridors**.
   - **[Ukraine–Spain Collaboration](https://www.railway.supply/ukrzaliznytsia-together-with-adif-is-adapting-the-technology-for-automated-gauge-changing-systems)**.

#### **For Infrastructure Managers (ADIF, PKP, LTG, etc.)**
1. **Upgrade gauge-changing facilities** to **reduce transition times** (target: **<10 sec per wheelset**).
   - **[TRIA High-Speed Gauge Changer](https://www.triarail.com/i+d/cambiador/en)**.
2. **Electrify all HSR corridors at 25 kV AC** to **align with EU standards**.
   - **[Rail Baltica Electrification](https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/featured-projects/rail-baltica-connecting-baltic-states-rest-europe-high-speed-train_en)**.
3. **Plan mixed-gauge track** in **Lithuania/Latvia** for **legacy 1,520 mm services** post-Rail Baltica.
   - **[ERA Gauge Compatibility](https://www.era.europa.eu/system/files/2022-11/Analysis%201520%20OPE%20%28EN%29.pdf)**.
